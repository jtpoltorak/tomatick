How to use it

  1. Press Start and work on one thing until the timer ends.
  2. Take the short break. Step away from the screen if you can.
  3. After 4 focus sessions (you can change this), take a long break.

Tips

  • The timer keeps running when the popup is closed. The toolbar badge shows the minutes left.
  • Press Space to start or pause, and Alt+Shift+P to open Tomatick.
  • Click Focus, Short break, or Long break to jump to that timer.
  • Sounds, notifications, and the site blocker are off until you turn them on in Settings.
