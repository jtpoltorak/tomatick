Privacy policy

Tomatick doesn't collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There's no account, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.

What stays on your computer

  • Your settings and the timer's progress are saved with Chrome's built-in storage so the timer keeps going when the popup closes. If Chrome Sync is on, Google syncs your settings between your own signed-in browsers. They never reach me.
  • Alert sounds are generated on your device. The extension makes no network requests.
  • The optional site blocker compares the addresses of pages you open against your list of blocked sites, inside Chrome, while a focus session is running. It doesn't read page content, and it doesn't record or send your browsing history anywhere. Chrome asks your permission when you turn it on, and Tomatick gives that permission back when you turn it off.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Tomatick deletes everything it stored.

Email

If you email me, I'll use your address and message only to reply to you, and I won't share them.

Children

Tomatick collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here and in the extension's store listing, with a new date at the top.
